Syllabus for TG EAPCET 2026-E Stream (Engineering Stream) 
1 
 
The syllabus is in tune with the syllabus adapted by the Board of Intermediate Education, TG, 
for Intermediate course with effect from the academic year 202 4-2025 (1st year) (100%) and 
2025-2026 (2nd year) (100%) and is designed at the level of Intermediate Course and equivalent 
to (10+2) scheme of Examination conducted by Board of Intermediate Education, T elangana. 
The syllabus is designed to indicate the scope of subjects included for TG EAPCET-2026. The 
topics mentioned therein are not to be regarded as exhaustive. Questions may be asked in T G 
EAPCET-2026 to test the student’s knowledge and intelligent understanding of the subject. 
The syllabus is applicable to students of both the current and previous batches of Intermediate 
Course, who desire to appear for TG EAPCET-2026.
