===== M-10-Q2 mathematics Permutation of distinct objects
The sum of all the 4 digits numbers formed using digits 0,2,3,5 (repetition is not allowed) is equal to
{'A': '64440', 'B': '63330', 'C': '62220', 'D': '66440'}
ANS A
SOL Sum of all four digit numbers formed = sum of all numbers - sum of numbers with 0 in the first place Sum of all the numbers = 6(2+3+5+0)(1000+100+10+1)= 66660 Sum of numbers with 0 in the first place = 2(2+3+5) (100+10+1) = 2220 Hence, the answer = 66660 - 2220 = 64440
